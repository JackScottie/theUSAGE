# Roadmap

## Delivered: first complete local play loop
- Challenge → bounded inventory → editable map → structural check → human observation → score → revision → portable record.
- Optional constrained AI solver and independent human/opponent attempts.
- Offline examples and manual authoring with explicit provenance.

## Next: evidence quality
- Physically test every example, including failure conditions and quantities.
- Version challenge definitions and store evidence attachments.
- Formalize consumable budgets, object states, transformations, and prerequisite capabilities.
- Extract reusable patterns such as bridging, stiffening, clamping, and absorption.

## Then: evaluation and reach
- Fixed-seed challenge generation and benchmark suites.
- Blind human judging of feasibility and ingenuity across model outputs.
- Custom object definitions and image-assisted inventory with human-confirmed measurements.
- Shared notebook or hosted play only after choosing identity, persistence, and secret management.
- Simulation/robotics execution after the planning semantics and evidence model mature.

## Archive
Recover original Wikipedia toolbook corpus, templates, or datastore if available. Keep original Python source and preserved faithful replica. Archival recovery is independent of the modern game.
