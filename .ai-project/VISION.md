# Vision

Usage Maps helps humans and AI connect the uses of available objects to achieve complex goals, including unconventional substitutions and assemblies.

The core artifact is an inspectable map: object properties → actions → intermediate states → measurable goal. A constrained invention game supplies goals, bounded resources, and opportunities to test whether the connections work.

AI proposes. Structural checks inspect declared resources and dependencies. People test physical feasibility and record evidence. Hypotheses, structural validity, and reported observations stay distinct.

The original robotics ambition remains a future execution layer. A human-operated tabletop workbench is the starting point for collecting reusable, tested usage relationships.
